Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore my purchases on a new device?

Settings → Restore Purchases. Sign in with the same Apple ID. Cozy Pass and Remove Ads will be restored.

How do I cancel my Cozy Pass subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not Cozy Florist:

  • Open the iOS Settings app.
  • Tap your name → Subscriptions.
  • Find Cozy Florist: Cozy Pass → Cancel Subscription.

I want a refund.

Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, find the purchase, and request a refund.

My idle progress isn't accumulating offline.

Make sure you've granted background app permissions and that your device is not in Low Power Mode. Idle progress accumulates while the app is closed for up to 8 hours by default — Cozy Pass extends this to 24 hours.

I lost my garden progress.

Progress is currently stored locally. Cloud sync is on the roadmap (target Q3 2026). Avoid uninstalling the app — uninstallation erases progress. If you've already uninstalled, contact us with your purchase receipt.

How do merge bonuses work?

Merge two flowers of the same level to create the next tier. Higher tiers produce more coins per harvest. Some rare flowers can be merged across categories — keep experimenting!

Is the game appropriate for kids?

Yes — rated 4+, no violence, no chat features, no UGC. Cozy and family-friendly.
