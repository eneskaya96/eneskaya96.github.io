Cozy Florist — Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 4, 2026 · Last updated: May 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how ASEN Games ("we", "our", "us") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the iOS app Cozy Florist ("the App").

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: None. The App does not require sign-up or account creation.
  • Personal information: None. The App does not collect names, emails, phone numbers, or location data.
  • Device identifiers: We may use a device identifier (such as Apple's IDFV or anonymized advertising identifier) for analytics and ad personalization, where you have consented.
  • Usage data: Anonymized gameplay events (level completion, session length, feature taps) are collected to improve game balance and identify bugs.
  • Diagnostics: Crash reports and performance metrics may be collected automatically.

2. How We Use Information

  • To operate the App and deliver gameplay.
  • To analyze game balance, retention, and feature usage.
  • To diagnose crashes and improve performance.
  • To deliver and measure advertisements (where the user has not opted out).
  • To process in-app purchases (handled exclusively by Apple — see Section 4).

3. Third-Party Services

  • Apple App Store / StoreKit: All payments and subscription management. Apple Privacy Policy
  • Apple App Analytics: Anonymized aggregate metrics from Apple.
  • Crash and analytics SDKs: Industry-standard SDKs (e.g., Firebase Crashlytics, Apple SKAdNetwork) for diagnostics and ad attribution. Anonymized data only.
  • Ad networks: Personalized advertising can be disabled in Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising, or by purchasing the "Remove Ads" in-app option.

4. In-App Purchases

The App offers optional in-app purchases (Cozy Pass monthly subscription, Remove Ads, Hint/Boost packs, optional currency packs). All purchases are processed by Apple. We do not receive or store payment information. Subscription auto-renew, cancellation, and refund requests are managed through your Apple ID account settings.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is rated 4+. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us — we will promptly delete it.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (EEA, UK, California), you may have the right to access, correct, or delete data; object to or restrict processing; withdraw consent for personalized advertising; and file a complaint with your local data protection authority. Contact us to exercise these rights.

7. Data Retention

Anonymized analytics and diagnostic data may be retained for up to 24 months. Crash reports are retained as long as needed to diagnose issues.

8. International Transfers

Data may be processed in countries other than your own, including the United States. We use industry-standard safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses where required.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Effective date" reflects the latest version. Continued use constitutes acceptance.
