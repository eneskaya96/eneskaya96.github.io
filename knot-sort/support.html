Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore my purchases on a new device?

Open Knot Sort, go to Settings → Restore Purchases. Sign in with the same Apple ID you used to make the original purchase. Your Cozy Pass and Remove Ads will be restored automatically.

How do I cancel my Cozy Pass subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by Knot Sort. To cancel:

  • Open the Settings app on your device.
  • Tap your name at the top, then tap Subscriptions.
  • Find Knot Sort: Cozy Pass and tap Cancel Subscription.

Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period.

I want a refund — what do I do?

All refund requests are handled directly by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple ID, find the Knot Sort purchase, and request a refund. We do not have the ability to process refunds on Apple's behalf.

The audio isn't playing properly.

Check that:

  • Your device is not in silent mode (check the side switch on iPhone).
  • Volume is turned up.
  • Knot Sort is allowed to play sound (Settings → Knot Sort → Audio).
  • Try toggling sound off and on inside the game's Settings.

If the problem persists, please email us with your device model and iOS version.

I lost my progress.

Knot Sort progress is currently stored locally on your device. We're working on cloud sync (target Q3 2026). For now, please avoid deleting the app — uninstalling will erase your progress. If you've already uninstalled, contact us with your purchase receipt and we can attempt to assist with subscription/purchase restoration.

I have a level suggestion or feedback.

We love hearing from players! Email us — we read everything. Particularly helpful: which levels feel too hard or too easy, ASMR audio you'd like more of, and any cozy themes you'd like to see in upcoming weekly level packs.

Is the game appropriate for kids?

Yes — Knot Sort is rated 4+ and contains no violence, mature themes, or chat features. It's designed to be calming and family-friendly.
