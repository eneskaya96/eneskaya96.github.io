Knot Sort — Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 3, 2026 · Last updated: May 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how ASEN Games ("we", "our", "us") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the iOS app Knot Sort: Yarn Untangle ASMR ("the App").

1. Information We Collect

We are committed to collecting only what we need to operate and improve the App.

  • Account information: None. The App does not require sign-up or account creation.
  • Personal information: None. The App does not collect names, emails, phone numbers, or location data.
  • Device identifiers: We may use a device identifier (such as Apple's IDFV or anonymized advertising identifier) for analytics and ad personalization, where you have consented.
  • Usage data: Anonymized gameplay events (level completion, session length, feature taps) are collected to improve game balance and identify bugs. This data is not linked to your real identity.
  • Diagnostics: Crash reports and performance metrics may be collected automatically.

2. How We Use Information

  • To operate the App and deliver the gameplay experience.
  • To analyze game balance, retention, and feature usage.
  • To diagnose crashes and improve performance.
  • To deliver and measure advertisements (where the user has not opted out).
  • To process in-app purchases (handled exclusively by Apple — see Section 4).

3. Third-Party Services

The App may use the following third-party services. Each operates under its own privacy policy.

  • Apple App Store / StoreKit: All payments and subscription management. Apple Privacy Policy
  • Apple App Analytics: Anonymized aggregate usage metrics provided to developers by Apple.
  • Crash and analytics providers: We may use industry-standard SDKs (e.g., Firebase Crashlytics, Apple SKAdNetwork) for diagnostics and ad attribution. These providers receive only anonymized data.
  • Ad networks (where ads are shown): We may use mobile ad networks. Personalized advertising can be disabled in your device settings (Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising) or by purchasing the "Remove Ads" in-app option.

4. In-App Purchases

The App offers optional in-app purchases (Cozy Pass monthly subscription, Remove Ads, Hint Pack). All purchases are processed by Apple. We do not receive or store your payment information. Subscription auto-renew, cancellation, and refund requests are managed through your Apple ID account settings.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is rated 4+ and is intended for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us using the email below and we will promptly delete it.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (including the EEA, UK, California), you may have the right to:

  • Access, correct, or delete data associated with your device identifier.
  • Object to or restrict our processing of your data.
  • Withdraw consent for personalized advertising at any time via your device settings.
  • File a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To exercise these rights, contact us at the email below.

7. Data Retention

Anonymized analytics and diagnostic data may be retained for up to 24 months. Crash reports are retained as long as needed to diagnose and fix the underlying issue.

8. International Transfers

Data may be processed in countries other than your own, including the United States. Where required, we use industry-standard safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses for international transfers.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Effective date" at the top of this page reflects the most recent version. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
